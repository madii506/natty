01 The lore

Nobody has ever proved anything.

He arrived on the sand in 1975 with a moustache, a chain and a tan, and he has not aged, shrunk or explained himself since. Four things are known.

01

The arrival

No gym bag, no training log, no coach. He was simply there one afternoon, already enormous, already tanned, already squinting into the middle distance behind a pair of aviators nobody has seen him remove.

02

The question

Somebody asked. He said he was natty. The asker said nothing for a long moment and then agreed that he was natty. This has happened roughly four thousand times and has never gone differently.

03

The traps

His ears have not been seen since the Carter administration. He maintains this is genetics. It is worth noting that nobody in his family has ears either, which he offers as proof and everybody else offers as evidence.

04

The coin

He has never taken anything. So the fee goes and buys the company that sells the thing he has never taken, and gives it to the people who believe him. If he is telling the truth, this is a very funny way to be wrong.

05 The pairing

He says he is natty. The fee buys HIMS anyway.

Every trade on this coin generates a creator fee. That fee buys tokenised HIMS — the listed company that sells the things a man takes when he is, let us say, not entirely natty — and it goes to the people holding $NATTY.

06 Questions

Is he natty?

Is he natty?

He is natty.

Seriously though

He has never failed a test, because he has never taken one, because nobody has ever organised one. In the only sense that can be evidenced, he is natty. In every other sense, look at the traps.

Why HIMS?

Because a coin about a man who insists he takes nothing is funniest when it quietly accumulates the company that sells the stuff. The stock is the punchline, not a sponsor — that is the whole design rule, and any pairing where the company is interchangeable is a worse idea.

Does the generator store my natty?

No. It draws on a canvas in your browser from a handful of numbers. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is saved, no wallet is touched, and the download is a PNG your machine made. Refresh the page and he is gone unless you kept the file.

Can I use the memes?

Yes. Take them, edit them, caption them, ruin them. Every image on this page was drawn for this coin and none of it is borrowed from anybody — which is exactly why it can be handed out.
